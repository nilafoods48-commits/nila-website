Child Safety Standards

These standards explain Vtok's commitment to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and protecting users from harmful activity.

1. About Vtok

Vtok is an adult social and dating application intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. Vtok does not permit minors to use the dating or social functionality of the service.

2. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Vtok has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Users must not create, upload, request, possess, share, distribute, promote, or facilitate child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any other content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers a child.

Prohibited behavior includes, but is not limited to:

  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming or attempting to establish contact with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Requesting, offering, sharing, or distributing sexual images involving minors.
  • Using Vtok to facilitate trafficking, exploitation, or abuse of children.
  • Attempting to evade age restrictions to access or contact minors.

3. Reporting Inside Vtok

Users can report another user, profile, message, image, or other content directly from the Vtok app. Reports may include a reason and additional details that help the safety team investigate.

If a user believes that content or behavior involves a child or possible CSAE/CSAM, the user should select the child-safety-related reporting reason and provide the relevant details.

4. Blocking

Vtok provides a blocking mechanism so users can prevent another account from contacting them or interacting with them. Blocking does not replace reporting when content or conduct may violate Vtok's safety standards.

5. How Vtok Handles Safety Reports

Vtok reviews reports using its safety and enforcement procedures. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, actions may include removing prohibited content, restricting or suspending an account, permanently banning an account, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and escalating matters to appropriate authorities.

When Vtok obtains actual knowledge of CSAM, Vtok will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and these standards, including removal or restriction of the material and account action where appropriate.

6. Child Safety Laws and Reporting to Authorities

Vtok maintains a process for handling confirmed CSAM and other child-safety matters in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements. Where legally required, confirmed CSAM or relevant information will be reported to the appropriate authority, which may include the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority.

Vtok may preserve and disclose information when required or permitted by law or valid legal process.

7. Child Safety Point of Contact

8. Enforcement

Violations of these standards may result in content removal, warnings, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, permanent account termination, and/or reporting to appropriate authorities where required or appropriate.

9. Contact and Additional Policies

For general support, users should use the support and reporting mechanisms available inside the Vtok app. Vtok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply to use of the service.

10. Policy Updates

Vtok may update these Child Safety Standards when its safety procedures, product features, or applicable legal and platform requirements change.
